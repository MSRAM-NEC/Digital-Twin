# Digital Twin Heart Project - Clinical Guidelines & Operational Protocol

## 1. Introduction
The Digital Twin Heart is an advanced physiological simulation platform designed to model the human cardiovascular system under various stress conditions and pharmacological interventions. It integrates multiple predictive layers: pharmacokinetics (PK), pharmacodynamics (PD), electrocardiogram (ECG) waveforms, and vital sign monitoring.

## 2. Drug Profiles and Standard Dosages

### 2.1 Metoprolol (Beta Blocker)
- **Mechanism:** Selectively blocks beta-1 adrenergic receptors in the heart.
- **Typical Dose:** 50 mg to 100 mg per day. Maximum recommended dose: 200 mg.
- **Expected Effects:** Decreases heart rate (Negative chronotropic) and reduces contractility (Negative inotropic).
- **Adverse Effects:** Bradycardia, hypotension, fatigue.

### 2.2 Lisinopril (ACE Inhibitor)
- **Mechanism:** Inhibits angiotensin-converting enzyme, preventing the conversion of angiotensin I to angiotensin II.
- **Typical Dose:** 10 mg to 40 mg once daily. Maximum recommended dose: 80 mg.
- **Expected Effects:** Lowers blood pressure through vasodilation. Decreases systemic vascular resistance (SVR).
- **Adverse Effects:** Dry cough, hyperkalemia, renal impairment in susceptible patients.

### 2.3 Amiodarone (Antiarrhythmic)
- **Mechanism:** Prolongs phase 3 of the cardiac action potential (potassium channel blockade).
- **Typical Dose:** 200 mg to 400 mg daily for maintenance. Maximum dose can safely be around 800 mg under extreme care.
- **Expected Effects:** Slows heart rate and AV node conduction. Widens QRS complex and prolongs QT interval on ECG.
- **Adverse Effects:** Pulmonary toxicity, thyroid dysfunction, liver toxicity.

### 2.4 Amlodipine (Calcium Channel Blocker)
- **Mechanism:** Inhibits calcium ion influx into vascular smooth muscle and cardiac muscle.
- **Typical Dose:** 5 mg to 10 mg once daily.
- **Expected Effects:** Vasodilation resulting in decreased blood pressure. Minimal effect on heart rate at typical doses.
- **Adverse Effects:** Peripheral edema, flushing, palpitations.

## 3. Machine Learning Heart Rate Prediction (Phase 2)
The Activity Heart Rate prediction engine uses a dual-model approach:
- **Ensemble Model:** Used for instant static heart rate point predictions based on user features (age, weight, height, resting HR).
- **LSTM Model:** Analyzes time-series data constraints to predict the trajectory of heart rate over extended physical routines.

## 4. Emergency Thresholds
Alert conditions trigger if simulated parameters cross the following thresholds:
- **Severe Bradycardia:** Heart Rate drops below 40 bpm.
- **Tachycardia:** Resting Heart Rate exceeds 120 bpm.
- **Hypertensive Crisis:** Systolic Blood Pressure > 180 mmHg or Diastolic > 120 mmHg.
- **Hypotension:** Systolic Blood Pressure < 90 mmHg.

## 5. Maintenance and Logging
All simulation runs are ephemeral by default. The platform logs prediction drift between the AI ensemble predictions and the theoretical heart block model outputs to track ongoing patient twin accuracy.
